Connect SoundCloud

Use Lyrion to find and play music. Connect your SoundCloud account here to save exact tracks to playlists.

1. Register your app

Open SoundCloud applications and register an application for Rabbit Hole. SoundCloud may require an Artist Pro subscription for API access.

Register this exact redirect address:

http://127.0.0.1:3777/api/soundcloud/callback

On the Rabbit Hole computer, add your app credentials to its local .env file, then restart Rabbit Hole:

SOUNDCLOUD_CLIENT_ID=your_client_id
SOUNDCLOUD_CLIENT_SECRET=your_client_secret
SOUNDCLOUD_REDIRECT_URI=http://127.0.0.1:3777/api/soundcloud/callback

Keep secrets in that file. Do not paste them into ChatGPT. Open this setup page on the Rabbit Hole computer to finish connecting with the default redirect.
