SiriusXM metadata

Live song, artist and show information is fetched automatically while you use SiriusXM in Lyrion.

No connection setup needed

Rabbit Hole reads SiriusXM’s timestamped live metadata feed. You do not need an activation code, Python, or a browser extension. You can close the SiriusXM browser tabs.

Lyrion continues to handle audio and playback controls. Metadata follows the live broadcast and may differ from delayed or buffered audio.

If live information is unavailable, Rabbit Hole falls back to public program information and Lyrion metadata.
